Welcome to Choice Clash! This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device ID, location, and app usage information to provide personalized ads. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

All app settings and game data are stored locally on your device. We do not store your personal data on external servers. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Our app is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us and we will remove it immediately.

Your Rights

You may choose to disable personalized advertising through your device settings at any time. You can also uninstall the app to stop all data collection.
